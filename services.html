Our Services

EHCMS gives Kenyans abroad and the Ministry of Foreign and Diaspora Affairs one secure place to handle consular and diaspora matters online. Through the platform, authorised officials and honorary consuls can manage their records, follow up on requests and communicate with the Ministry without the delay of paperwork, helping services reach the global Kenyan community faster and more reliably.
